# Bonsai First Aid Manual

## Directive
- Domain: home-gardening
- Action: plant-first-aid
- Triggers: bonsai, yellow leaves, root rot, watering, succulent, pothos, anthurium, snake plant, monstera
- Params: plant, symptom

## Knowledge

### Pothos
#### Yellow Leaves
- Cause: Overwatering or insufficient light.
- First Aid: Stop watering immediately, move to indirect light, trim yellow leaves. If soil is waterlogged, replace with dry potting mix.
- Prevention: Water once/week in spring/autumn, twice/week in summer, every 10 days in winter. Avoid direct sunlight.

#### Root Rot
- Signs: Roots turn black, soft, with foul odor.
- First Aid: Remove plant from pot, cut away rotted roots, soak in fungicide solution for 15 min, repot with fresh soil.
- Prevention: Use well-draining pots, add clay pebbles at bottom, water only when soil is dry.

### Succulents
#### Translucent Leaves
- Symptoms: Leaves turn transparent, fall off at slightest touch.
- First Aid: Stop watering, increase ventilation, remove affected leaves. In severe cases, unpot and air-dry for 2-3 days before repotting.
- Prevention: Provide shade in summer, use 70%+ gritty mix, water interval ≥ 10 days.

#### Etiolation
- Signs: Stem internodes elongate, leaves become sparse.
- First Aid: Gradually increase light (no sudden direct sun), behead and propagate for reshaping.
- Prevention: Ensure ≥ 4 hours of light daily, control watering frequency.

### Anthurium
#### Blackened Blooms
- Cause: Water splashed on blooms, or low humidity.
- First Aid: Remove blackened blooms, water only at the base. Increase humidity with a water tray or humidifier nearby.
- Prevention: Maintain humidity above 60%, avoid misting during flowering.

### Monstera
#### Crispy Leaf Edges
- Cause: Dry air or over-fertilization.
- First Aid: Trim burnt edges, flush soil with water to dilute fertilizer, increase misting.
- Prevention: Light feeding during growing season, stop fertilizing in winter, ensure good ventilation.

### Snake Plant
#### Soft, Drooping Leaves
- Cause: Overwatering leading to stem rot.
- First Aid: Stop watering immediately, remove plant and air-dry roots, cut away rotted parts, repot with dry soil.
- Prevention: Better too dry than too wet — water once a month in winter.
